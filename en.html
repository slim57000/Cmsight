What the software does

An assistant that knows your CMS

WordPress, WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Joomla, Drupal. It detects the CMS on connection and writes code that fits its structure, not generic snippets.

Preview before, backup during

You see the before/after diff of every file. When you apply, the original is copied aside: restore it in one click if you don't like the result.

Local, FTP or SFTP

A folder on your disk, shared hosting over FTP, or a server over SFTP. Setting up a connection takes two minutes.

No account, no cloud

Your sites, credentials and API keys stay on your PC. A password protects access at launch.

The app

Your sites stay with you.

Nothing is stored on a Cmsight server. Your site's files, API keys and FTP credentials never leave your machine, and no change is written without your approval.
